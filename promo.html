Questions

Before you pay.

The honest answers — including how payment actually works, and why nobody touches your money.

How do I pay, exactly?

Send $10 through PayPal, Cash App, Venmo, Zelle (by email only), or Revolut using the details in the Get SprayQuote section, and put “SprayQuote” in the payment note. There's no card checkout — payment is direct. After paying, you receive an unlock code to enter at PHXHQ.github.io/SprayQuote.

Is billing automated?

No. There's no Stripe, no automated checkout, and no automated billing — each month is paid the same direct way. This page doesn't promise any billing mechanic beyond that.

Does SprayQuote ever hold my customers' payments?

Never. You put your own payment links on the quote, and the customer pays you directly through them. No platform holds your funds, delays a payout, or takes a cut — nobody between you and your money.

What is the Phoenix Price Brief?

Every month, members get a fresh read on what exterior-cleaning jobs are actually going for around the Valley. It's published as ranges — not single prices — with a visible date stamp showing when it was last reviewed (October 2026), and it's folded into SprayQuote's shipped defaults. It's included in the $10/month price. Updates apply to the shipped defaults only: if you've set your own custom rates, they are never overwritten.

Why is there only one price?

Because one price, no ladder is the deal: everything SprayQuote does is in the $10/mo — no tiers to climb, no per-user tolls, no features held behind an add-on gate. What you see on this page is what the $10 unlocks.

What's the difference between the sample and the full tool?

The sample (code SAMPLE602) is a public, read-only preview of a finished quote so you can see what SprayQuote produces. The full unlock lets you build your own quotes with your services, rates, and branding, save them on your device, and send share links, signatures, and deposit links to real customers.

Who built SprayQuote?

Someone who runs a working wash-supply counter and talks with contractors daily — built by the trade, for the trade. It's a Phoenix product, shaped by how exterior cleaning work is actually quoted and sold in the Valley.

Do I have to enter my code every time?

No. After a successful unlock, the tool remembers it on that device and browser for 7 days and restores the same access level. After 7 days it asks again — and on a different device or after clearing browser data, you'll enter your code again.

Where are my quotes saved?

On the device you're using. Your saved quotes and settings stay on-device, so they're there when you reopen SprayQuote on that same device and browser.

Does it handle tax for my area?

SprayQuote includes city tax for Phoenix-metro cities and applies it to the quote by job city. If you work outside the Phoenix metro, check the tax on your quotes against your local rate.

Can I use it for commercial jobs?

Yes — SprayQuote prices both residential and commercial exterior cleaning jobs, with customizable services so you can set it up for the work you actually sell.
